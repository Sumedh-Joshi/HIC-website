Projects

Travel Health Advisory

Event: Kent Hack Enough 2025

Travel Health Advisory is a platform designed to help travelers stay informed about health risks when visiting different countries. The system integrates map-based visualizations and an AI-powered chatbot to provide real-time health information, safety insights, and travel recommendations.

Built during Kent Hack Enough 2025, this project won the AI Track.

Trially

Trially is a healthcare platform built during a hackathon to help patients find and match with relevant clinical trials using AI-driven analysis of medical eligibility criteria. The project aims to simplify clinical trial discovery and improve patient participation in research studies.
